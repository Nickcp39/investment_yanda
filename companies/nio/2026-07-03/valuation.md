## Rough framing

Q1 2026 revenue was US$3.70B. A simple annualized Q1 run rate is about US$14.8B, while Q2 revenue guidance was US$4.75B to US$4.99B. Against a market cap around US$12B, NIO screens cheaply on sales.

The reason it screens cheap is also the risk: the market is not paying for revenue alone. It is demanding evidence that revenue can turn into durable GAAP profit and free cash flow.

## Multiples that matter

Do not anchor only on P/S. For NIO, the key valuation bridge should be:

- deliveries,
- ASP,
- vehicle margin,
- gross profit,
- R&D + SG&A leverage,
- operating profit,
- operating cash flow,
- maintenance / growth capex,
- diluted share count.

## Scenario sketch

### Bear

Revenue grows, but vehicle margin falls to low teens or worse. Operating losses return. Equity remains a trading vehicle and gets a low P/S multiple.

### Base

Deliveries keep growing, vehicle margin stabilizes in mid-teens, adjusted profit is roughly break-even to modestly positive, and cash burn is controlled. Stock can rerate but remains volatile.

### Bull

NIO proves mid/high-teens vehicle margin, power/service losses narrow, operating cash flow turns durable positive, and the three-brand platform works without brand dilution. The market stops valuing NIO as a distressed EV startup and starts valuing it as a profitable China EV platform.

## Current valuation stance

Cheap-looking but not automatically cheap.

NIO needs a profit-quality trigger. Without Q2/Q3 margin proof, this is closer to an operating inflection watchlist name than a conviction compounder.
