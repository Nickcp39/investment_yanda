# Bottleneck Map

## Current bottlenecks

1. Margin durability.
2. Capital intensity.
3. China EV price war.
4. Multi-brand execution.
5. Battery swap utilization economics.
6. Geopolitical / ADR sentiment.

## Bottleneck ranking

### 1. Margin durability

This is the most important bottleneck. NIO's Q1 margin improvement is meaningful. But if Q2/Q3 show lower margins after ONVO/FIREFLY mix rises, the thesis weakens quickly.

### 2. Capital intensity

EVs require constant platform refreshes, software investment, manufacturing capacity, service network, and energy infrastructure. NIO must show it can grow without repeated external capital.

### 3. Competitive pricing

BYD, Tesla, Xiaomi, Li Auto, XPeng, and legacy automakers can force price pressure. NIO needs differentiation strong enough to avoid competing purely on price.

### 4. Product cycle

The current product cycle is strong. The risk is that investor expectations already price in the product cycle before the financials confirm it.

### 5. Geopolitical / listing risk

The DoD CMC list issue is not presented by NIO as a sanctions or trading restriction, but it adds headline and institutional ownership risk.

## What would remove the bottleneck?

- Q2/Q3 vehicle margin stays near or above mid-teens.
- Operating cash flow positive.
- GAAP operating profit or near break-even with clear bridge.
- Swap/power solutions losses narrow.
- Brand mix disclosed with stable ASP and margin.
