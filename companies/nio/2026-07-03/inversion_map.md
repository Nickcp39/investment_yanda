# Inversion Map

## How the bullish thesis breaks

### Failure mode 1: delivery growth without profit

NIO sells more cars, but gross margin falls as ONVO/FIREFLY mix rises and competitors cut prices. Revenue grows, but owner earnings do not.

### Failure mode 2: swap network remains a cost center

Customers like battery swap, but station utilization and pricing do not support attractive returns on capital.

### Failure mode 3: product-cycle peak

2026 delivery growth reflects a fresh model cycle. Once the cycle normalizes, volume and ASP fade while fixed costs remain.

### Failure mode 4: capital markets penalty

U.S.-China risk, DoD list headlines, ADR discount, or dilution caps the equity multiple even if operations improve.

### Failure mode 5: BYD/Tesla/Xiaomi squeeze

Cost leaders and brand leaders set pricing lower. NIO is stuck in the middle: not the cheapest, not the global AI/autonomy narrative leader, and not consistently profitable.

## What would prove bears wrong

- Two to four quarters of stable vehicle margin despite lower-price brand mix.
- Positive operating cash flow.
- No major dilution.
- Service/power economics visibly improve.
- Premium NIO brand keeps pricing power while ONVO/FIREFLY add scale.
- Product cycle continues without heavy discounting.

## Kill criteria

- Vehicle margin drops below 12% without a credible temporary explanation.
- Q2/Q3 revenue misses while inventories rise.
- Operating cash flow materially negative again.
- Financing risk returns despite RMB48.2B liquidity.
- Regulatory/listing risk starts affecting capital access or institutional ownership.
