# Monitor

## Next events

- Q2 2026 financial results.
- July / August / September 2026 monthly deliveries.
- Brand-level delivery mix for NIO, ONVO, FIREFLY.
- Any update on DoD CMC list removal or legal challenge.

## Monthly dashboard

Track:

- Total monthly deliveries.
- NIO brand deliveries.
- ONVO deliveries.
- FIREFLY deliveries.
- Lower-price brand share = (ONVO + FIREFLY) / total.
- Estimated ASP if disclosed or inferable.
- Competitor deliveries: BYD, Li Auto, XPeng, Tesla China, Xiaomi.
- Stock reaction to delivery beats/misses.

## Quarterly dashboard

Track:

- Vehicle margin.
- Gross margin.
- Operating income/loss.
- Adjusted operating income.
- Net income/loss.
- Operating cash flow.
- Free cash flow.
- Inventory.
- Payables.
- Cash/liquidity.
- Diluted share count.

## Upgrade triggers

- Q2/Q3 vehicle margin holds near or above mid-teens.
- GAAP operating loss continues to narrow or turns positive.
- Operating cash flow turns positive.
- No material dilution.
- Lower-price brands scale while premium NIO brand stays strong.

## Downgrade triggers

- Delivery growth slows while discounts rise.
- Vehicle margin drops sharply.
- Product-cycle demand disappoints.
- Battery swap economics remain opaque or worsen.
- Geopolitical/listing risk intensifies.
