# NIO Memo v1

Date: 2026-07-03
Status: first-pass pipeline memo
Decision: WATCH

## Thesis

NIO is an operating inflection candidate, not yet a proven compounder. The company has improved enough that it deserves fresh work: Q1 2026 vehicle margin reached 18.8%, adjusted net profit was slightly positive, liquidity was RMB48.2B, and Q2 deliveries grew 49.4% YoY. The business is no longer only a dream-stock story.

But the core question is still unresolved: can NIO convert multi-brand scale into durable owner earnings in China's EV price war?

## What changed

The important change is not only delivery growth. It is the combination of:

- stronger Q1 vehicle margin,
- much narrower operating loss,
- adjusted operating and adjusted net profit,
- active 2026 product cycle,
- three-brand architecture,
- higher June delivery run-rate.

That combination makes NIO worth monitoring more closely than during its pure cash-burn phase.

## The problem

Q2 delivery actual was 107,658, below the 110,000 to 115,000 guidance given with Q1 results. That is not fatal, but it matters because NIO's stock needs confidence in execution. At this stage, a small miss can make investors ask whether demand, production, or pricing is weaker than hoped.

The other problem is mix. In June, ONVO plus FIREFLY were about 46% of deliveries. This can create scale, but it may also lower ASP and pressure margin. We need Q2/Q3 financials to see whether this mix is good scale or expensive volume.

## What I like

- Vehicle margin recovery is real.
- Liquidity is strong enough to give management time.
- Product cycle appears active across multiple brands.
- NIO still has a differentiated user/service/swap identity.
- If Q2/Q3 financials confirm margin durability, the valuation could rerate from "distressed EV" toward "profitable platform."

## What I do not like

- GAAP net loss still exists.
- Q2 deliveries missed guidance.
- China EV competition is extremely hard.
- Swap network economics are not yet proven enough for a moat claim.
- Lower-price brands could dilute economics.
- ADR/geopolitical risk is now more visible after the DoD CMC list issue.

## Decision

WATCH.

I would not treat NIO as a high-conviction buy yet. The setup is interesting because the stock looks cheap on sales and the operations are improving. But the correct next step is to wait for Q2 financial quality: vehicle margin, gross margin, operating cash flow, and inventory/payables.

If Q2 confirms that 100k+ quarterly deliveries can coexist with mid/high-teens vehicle margin, the thesis becomes much more interesting. If margins crack, then the delivery growth is lower quality and the stock remains a trading vehicle.

## What would make me change my mind

Upgrade:

- Q2/Q3 vehicle margin remains near or above mid-teens.
- Operating cash flow improves materially.
- GAAP losses continue to shrink.
- ONVO/FIREFLY scale without hurting NIO premium brand.

Downgrade:

- Vehicle margin drops below low-teens.
- Delivery growth is bought with discounts.
- Cash flow worsens despite higher volume.
- Guidance misses continue.
- Geopolitical/listing risk starts to affect capital access.
