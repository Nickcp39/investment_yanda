# Financial Quality

## Snapshot

NIO's financial quality is improving but not yet clean. The headline positive is the Q1 2026 margin/profitability inflection. The headline caution is that GAAP profitability and free cash flow durability are still unproven.

## Margin

Vehicle margin improved to 18.8% in Q1 2026, compared with 10.2% in Q1 2025 and 18.1% in Q4 2025. Gross margin improved to 19.0%, compared with 7.6% in Q1 2025 and 17.5% in Q4 2025.

This is a real improvement. It suggests product mix, scale, and cost discipline were all working in Q1. The next test is whether this margin level survives a higher ONVO/FIREFLY mix.

## Profitability

Q1 2026:

- Operating loss: RMB308.8M.
- Adjusted operating profit: RMB66.8M.
- Net loss: RMB332.1M.
- Adjusted net profit: RMB43.5M.

Interpretation: NIO is near break-even on an adjusted basis, but not yet safely profitable. A small adverse mix shift, incentive increase, or volume miss could move it back into larger losses.

## Balance sheet

Liquidity was RMB48.2B at 2026-03-31. That gives NIO runway and lowers near-term financing risk. But liquidity alone is not a moat. The question is whether the business can fund product cycles, swap infrastructure, and overseas expansion internally.

## Revenue quality

Q1 2026 revenue rose 112.2% YoY but fell 26.3% QoQ. The YoY growth looks strong because Q1 2025 was weak; the QoQ decline shows the business still has product-cycle and seasonal volatility.

## Red flags to test

- Inventory build if deliveries slow.
- Payables extension or supplier financing pressure.
- Rising discounts hidden inside ASP.
- Lower brand mix dragging vehicle margin below mid-teens.
- Power solution losses if swap utilization lags.
- Equity issuance if losses return.

## Current financial-quality grade

B- / improving, still cyclical and execution-heavy.

This is not yet the profile of a proven compounder. It is closer to a turnaround / operating inflection candidate.
