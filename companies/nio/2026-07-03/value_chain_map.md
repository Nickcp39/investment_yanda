# Value Chain Map

## EV value chain

1. Raw materials: lithium, nickel, graphite, copper, aluminum, rare earths.
2. Battery cells and packs: chemistry, cell cost, pack integration, safety.
3. Vehicle platform: chassis, drivetrain, electronics, thermal system.
4. Manufacturing: scale, quality, yield, supplier management.
5. Software and intelligence: ADAS, OS, cockpit, OTA, data loop.
6. Energy / charging / battery swap: user convenience and infrastructure economics.
7. Brand / distribution / service: trust, service quality, community, residual value.
8. Financing / leasing / used vehicles: affordability and lifecycle monetization.

## Where NIO tries to differentiate

- Premium user experience and community.
- Battery swap and BaaS-like flexibility.
- Multi-brand segmentation.
- Smart driving / NIO WorldModel / in-house chip efforts.
- Service layer and after-sales monetization.

## Where NIO is structurally exposed

- Battery cells and commodities are not fully controlled.
- China EV pricing is set by the fiercest competitors, not by NIO alone.
- Premium EV demand can be cyclical.
- Lower-price brands may make scale easier but premium economics harder.
- Swap infrastructure requires dense demand and utilization.

## Bottleneck today

The bottleneck is not technology ambition. It is proof that the system can scale profitably in the market as it exists, not the market NIO wishes existed.
