# Step 0 Plan

## Question

Can NIO become a durable, profitable EV platform, or is it a cyclical rebound inside a structurally overcrowded China EV market?

## Primary hypothesis

The bullish version is that NIO has crossed from expensive dream stock into a real operating inflection: multi-brand scale, better product cycle, premium SUV strength, service monetization, and improved cost discipline.

## Main disconfirming hypothesis

The bearish version is that the improvement is a volume/mix cycle that can reverse quickly. ONVO and FIREFLY expand deliveries but dilute ASP and margins, while swap infrastructure and R&D keep capital intensity high. If China EV pricing remains deflationary, NIO may look cheap on sales but not cheap on normalized owner earnings.

## Work plan

1. Establish hard facts from official sources.
2. Separate delivery growth from economic quality.
3. Map the value chain and identify where NIO can actually earn excess returns.
4. Build risk inversion: what would make this a value trap?
5. Build monitoring list for Q2/Q3 2026.
6. Defer valuation conclusion until current price, diluted ADS count, and Q2 financials are refreshed.
