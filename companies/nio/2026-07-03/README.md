Date: 2026-07-03

## One-minute view

NIO is no longer only a premium EV brand story. The current underwriting question is whether NIO can turn a three-brand portfolio (NIO / ONVO / FIREFLY), battery swap, software, services, and premium community into durable owner earnings in the middle of China's brutal EV competition.

My current stance is WATCH, not core position. The business has materially improved: Q1 2026 vehicle margin reached 18.8%, Q1 adjusted net profit was positive, and Q2 deliveries grew 49.4% YoY. But the quality still depends on proof that the June/Q2 volume can convert into GAAP earnings, free cash flow, and durable margins after ONVO/FIREFLY mix increases.

## Current verdict

WATCH / evidence gap.

NIO is worth monitoring because the operating inflection is real enough to study. It is not yet clean enough to underwrite as a high-quality compounding business, because the next proof point is not deliveries alone. The proof point is whether Q2 and Q3 show that higher volume and lower-price brands preserve vehicle margin, reduce operating loss, and produce operating cash flow without leaning on dilution or supplier financing.
