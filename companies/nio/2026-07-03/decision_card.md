# Decision Card - NIO

Date: 2026-07-03

## Decision

WATCH.

## Why

NIO has a real operating improvement signal, especially Q1 2026 vehicle margin and adjusted profitability. But it has not yet proven durable GAAP profit or free cash flow through a higher ONVO/FIREFLY mix.

## Scorecard

| Dimension | Score | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Growth | 8/10 | Q2 2026 deliveries +49.4% YoY |
| Margin trend | 7/10 | Q1 vehicle margin 18.8%, needs Q2 proof |
| Financial quality | 5/10 | adjusted profit positive, GAAP still loss |
| Moat | 5/10 | swap/brand/software differentiated but economics unproven |
| Balance sheet | 7/10 | RMB48.2B liquidity |
| Competitive position | 5/10 | China EV market remains extremely competitive |
| Valuation | 6/10 | cheap on sales, not yet cheap on proven earnings |
| Geopolitical/listing risk | 4/10 | DoD CMC list headline risk |

## Upgrade condition

Upgrade to DEEP_DIVE / starter candidate if Q2 or Q3 shows high-teens or mid-teens vehicle margin, improving operating cash flow, and no evidence that delivery growth is being bought with discounts.

## Downgrade condition

Downgrade if vehicle margin drops sharply, Q3 guide disappoints, inventory/payables worsen, or geopolitical risk starts affecting ownership/liquidity.
