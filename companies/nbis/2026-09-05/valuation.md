And the prior card's own clarification, carried because it is the right reasoning:

> Rising price per MW is **not** a falsification of the capital-cycle lens. High returns attracting
> capital is the **first step** of that cycle, not its opposite. The variable that decides the question is
> the **supply-response lead time** — transformers and gas generators being severely short. That lead time
> is the term of the excess return.
