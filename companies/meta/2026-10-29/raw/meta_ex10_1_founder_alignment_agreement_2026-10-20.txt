EXHIBIT 10.1
META PLATFORMS, INC.
FOUNDER ALIGNMENT AGREEMENT
This Founder Alignment Agreement (this “Agreement”) is made as of October 20, 2026 (the “Execution Date”), by and between Meta Platforms, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), and Mark Zuckerberg (the “Founder” and, together with the Company, the “Parties”). Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined have the meaning set forth in Section 1.
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of the Company, acting upon the recommendation of a special committee of the Board duly appointed pursuant to Section 144 of the DGCL and comprised of disinterested directors with respect to matters set forth in this Agreement, and the Company’s stockholders have approved the adoption of an Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation of the Company (the “Amended & Restated Charter”) to authorize the creation of a non-voting class of common stock of the Company, par value $0.000006 per share, designated as “Class C Common Stock” (the “Class C Common Stock”), to be filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware no later than two (2) business days after the Execution Date and effective January 19, 2027 (the “Amended & Restated Charter Effective Date”);
WHEREAS, the Board has declared a dividend of one (1) newly issued share of Class C Common Stock on each outstanding share of the Company’s Class A Common Stock, par value $0.000006 per share (the “Class A Common Stock”), and each outstanding share of the Company’s Class B Common Stock, par value $0.000006 per share (the “Class B Common Stock”), as of a record date the Board will determine in good faith therefor (the “Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend”), with the payment of such dividend to be made no later than the fifth business day following the Amended & Restated Charter Effective Date;
WHEREAS, a condition to the payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend is that this Agreement continues to be a valid and binding obligation of the Parties in full force and effect as of the payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend;
WHEREAS, the Company expects to issue shares of Class C Common Stock from time to time to raise capital, to fund acquisitions, to compensate employees, and for other general corporate purposes, each of which will dilute the economic interest of every stockholder, including the Founder;
WHEREAS, the Board has considered from time to time whether, in addition to the actions described above, it would be advisable and in the best interests of the Company to further the alignment between the interests of the Founder and the Company’s other stockholders by issuing to the Founder equity or equity-based incentive awards or other compensatory instruments of the kind granted to the Company’s other executive officers and/or of the kind granted to executive officers of certain of the Company’s peer companies, which would entail compensation expense and dilution borne by all of the Company’s stockholders;
WHEREAS, previously the Board, acting through its Compensation, Nominating & Governance Committee, determined, as disclosed in the Company’s annual proxy statements, that the Founder’s historical equity ownership sufficiently aligned his interests with those of the Company’s other stockholders such that no equity or equity-based incentive awards or other compensatory instruments were warranted, and since 2013, the Founder has not received any such award or other compensatory instrument;
WHEREAS, the Board has determined that the alignment between the interests of the Founder and the Company’s other stockholders is best preserved, and compensation expense and dilution of all of the Company stockholders are best avoided, through the Founder’s continued executive service at the Company and his retention of a substantial economic ownership interest in addition to his majority voting interest;
WHEREAS, the Founder will receive the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend and any Additional Dividend solely in his capacity as a stockholder, pro rata with and on the same terms as every other holder of Class A Common Stock and Class B Common Stock (other than as set forth in this Agreement); and
WHEREAS, in furtherance of the foregoing and as a condition to the payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend, the Founder has agreed, on behalf of himself and the Holder Group, to the restrictions on the Sale of Stapled Class
C Shares set forth herein, including a requirement, commencing on the payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend and subject to the terms set forth herein, that the Holder Group must Sell or convert a proportionate number of shares of Class B Common Stock or Sell a proportionate number of shares of Class A Common Stock, or apply Stapling Credits, for every Sale of Stapled Class C Shares by the Holder Group as set forth herein, and the Founder has agreed to the further covenants set forth in Sections 4, 6 and 8.
AGREEMENT

“IRR” shall mean, as of any measurement date, the compound annualized shareholder return for the period from the Initial 30-Day Trailing Average Price Reference Date through such measurement date calculated on a per-share basis using the equitably adjusted Initial 30-Day Trailing Average Price (i.e., $290.30 per share) as the initial value (subject to the pre-Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend adjustment described in clause (i) below) and giving effect to the reinvestment of cash dividends and other cash distributions as set forth below, determined assuming that:
(i)prior to the date of payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend, each cash dividend or other cash distribution with an ex-dividend date during the measurement period shall be reinvested on such ex-dividend date in additional fractional shares of Class A Common Stock at the closing price of Class A Common Stock as of such ex-dividend date, in each case calculated without giving effect to the equitable adjustment for the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend (such that the initial value for purposes of such calculation shall be the Initial 30-Day Trailing Average Price), and, upon payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend, all applicable per-share values shall be equitably adjusted to reflect the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend;
(ii)after the date of payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend, each cash dividend or other cash distribution with an ex-dividend date during the measurement period is reinvested on such ex-dividend date in additional fractional shares of Class C Common Stock at the closing price of Class C Common Stock as of such ex-dividend date;
(iii)the value of such notional investment as of the measurement date will be calculated assuming ownership of one (1) share of Class A Common Stock deemed initially held based on the equitably adjusted Initial 30-Day
Trailing Average Price of $290.30 per share, and all additional shares and fractional shares acquired through such reinvestment, multiplied by the 30-Day Trailing Average Price as of such measurement date; and
(iv)such total return is annualized by raising (a) the quotient obtained by dividing (x) such value in the preceding clause (iii) by (y) the equitably adjusted Initial 30-Day Trailing Average Price to (b) a power equal to (x) 365 divided by (y) the number of calendar days elapsed in such period and (c) subtracting one (1); provided that all applicable share prices and per-share cash dividends or distributions shall be equitably adjusted to reflect the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend and any stock split, stock dividend, recapitalization, spin-off, split-off or similar event.

“Unstapled Class C Shares” shall mean any shares of Class C Common Stock that are not Stapled Class C Shares.

2.Sales of Class C Common Stock.
(a)From and after the payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend, the Founder shall not, and shall cause each member of the Holder Group not to, Sell any Stapled Class C Shares, unless, in connection with any Sale of Stapled Class C Shares, (i) a member of the Holder Group, no later than two (2) business days after the settlement date of such Sale, either (A) Sells a number of shares of Class A Common Stock or Class B Common Stock, or (B) converts a number of shares of Class B Common Stock into shares of Class A Common Stock, or any combination thereof, in each case of clause (A) and (B), equal in the aggregate to the product (rounded up to the nearest whole share) of the number of Stapled Class C Shares so Sold and the Unstapling Ratio, or (ii) Stapling Credits equal to the product of the number of Stapled Class C Shares so Sold and the Unstapling Ratio are applied to such Sale pursuant to Section 2(d), it being agreed that, unless otherwise designated by the Founder, any Stapling Credits then accrued and not previously applied shall automatically be applied to any Sale at a rate of a number of Stapling Credits equal to the Unstapling Ratio per Stapled Class C Share Sold, until all such Stapling Credits have been exhausted, and the requirements of Section 2(a)(i) shall apply only to Stapled Class C Shares Sold in such Sale for which no Stapling Credit remains available.
(b)The Company shall not (i) instruct or permit its transfer agent to process the de-legending of any Stapled Class C Shares in violation of this Agreement or (ii) preclear or approve any 10b5-1 trading arrangement providing for any Sale of Stapled Class C Shares in violation of this Agreement. The Stapled Class C Shares held by the Holder Group will be subject to restrictive legends, stop transfer orders, or other appropriate instructions in place with the Company’s transfer agent. In furtherance of the foregoing, the Company shall cooperate promptly with the Holder Group and instruct its transfer agent as necessary to promptly remove such restrictive legends, stop transfer orders, or instructions, as applicable, with respect to any (a) Stapled Class C Shares that are Sold by any member of the Holder Group in compliance with this Agreement (including by the application of Stapling Credits pursuant to Section 2(d)) or (b) Stapled Class C Shares that become Unstapled Class C Shares pursuant to the terms of this Agreement, and enable the Holder Group to settle such transactions promptly; provided that to the extent any Stapled Class C Shares are held in a brokerage account or by a bank or other nominee (i.e., in “street name”), the Founder agrees, and agrees to cause the Holder Group, to provide the Company with reasonably current updates regarding account statements of such holdings, so that the Company may ensure compliance hereunder, and Founder agrees to direct its broker or bank or other nominee to comply with the terms of this Agreement.
(c)Stapling Condition; Automatic Conversion.
(i)Subject to Section 2(c)(iv), if at any time a member of the Holder Group Sells Stapled Class C Shares in violation of Section 2(a), then the Holder Group shall be deemed to have irrevocably converted a number of shares of Class B Common Stock owned by the Holder Group equal to the product (rounded up to the nearest whole share) of (A) the number of such Stapled Class C Shares Sold in violation of Section 2(a) and (B) the Unstapling Ratio, automatically and without any further action, into an equal number of fully paid and nonassessable shares of Class A Common Stock, with such conversion allocated among the
Holder Group as designated by the Founder from time to time. Upon any such conversion of shares of Class B Common Stock to Class A Common Stock, all rights of the applicable member of the Holder Group of such shares of Class B Common Stock shall cease and the applicable member of the Holder Group in whose name or names the shares of Class A Common Stock are to be issued shall be treated for all purposes as having become the record holder or holders of such shares of Class A Common Stock.
(ii)On behalf of each member of the Holder Group, the Founder authorizes the Company and its transfer agent to take any and all actions that may be necessary to cause any such conversion to occur, whether or not such member of the Holder Group has taken any action with respect thereto, including the Company providing appropriate instruction on behalf of such member of the Holder Group to its transfer agent to effect such conversion. On behalf of the Holder Group, the Founder agrees that the automatic conversion set forth in Section 2(c)(i) above shall apply to the shares of Class B Common Stock held by the Holder Group as designated by the Founder or, if the Founder fails to so designate within five (5) business days of receiving notice from the Company of the failure to comply with Section 2(a), (1) first, to shares of Class B Common Stock owned beneficially and of record by the member of the Holder Group that is Selling Stapled Class C Shares and (2) for any remaining Stapled Class C Shares that remain unaccounted for, to shares of Class B Common Stock owned beneficially and of record by the other members of the Holder Group proportionately based on the number of shares of Class B Common Stock then held by each such member of the Holder Group, or in accordance with any other order that the Founder has designated from time to time.
(iii)The Founder shall, and shall cause each member of the Holder Group to, cooperate fully with the Company in connection with any conversion pursuant to Section 2(c)(i), and shall take all such actions as may be necessary or desirable to cause the documentation and implementation of such conversion as promptly as practicable, including delivering, or causing to be delivered, to the Company’s transfer agent such documentation as the transfer agent may reasonably require.
(iv)Notwithstanding Section 2(c)(i), if any Stapled Class C Shares are Sold in violation of Section 2(a) as a result of an inadvertent error (including any error by any member of the Holder Group or any of their respective brokers, custodians or other agents, the transfer agent or the Company), no conversion of shares of Class B Common Stock shall occur under this Section 2(c) in respect of such Stapled Class C Shares, and such Sale shall be deemed for all purposes of this Agreement to have been made in compliance with Section 2(a) and shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement, if, prior to the expiration of the period ending five (5) trading days after the earlier of (1) the date on which the Company gives the Founder written notice of such Sale, which the Company shall promptly do upon being made aware of the Sale, and (2) the date on which the Founder gives the Company written notice of such Sale, which the Founder shall promptly do upon being made aware of the Sale (such period, the “Cure Period”), the Holder Group (A) Sells a number of shares of Class A Common Stock or Class B Common Stock, or converts a number of shares of Class B Common Stock into shares of Class A Common Stock, or any combination thereof, in the aggregate amount as would have been required in connection with a compliant Sale pursuant to Section 2(a)(i), (B) causes such Sale to be rescinded or unwound such that the Holder Group again owns such Stapled Class C Shares, beneficially and of record, or (C) designates a number of Unstapled Class C Shares equal to the number of such Stapled Class C Shares Sold in violation of Section 2(a) as Stapled Class C Shares for all purposes of this Agreement, in which case the Stapled Class C Shares so Sold shall be deemed to have been Unstapled Class C Shares at the time of such Sale. The foregoing shall apply only if, prior to the expiration of the Cure Period, the Founder delivers to the Company written notice describing such Sale of Stapled Class C Shares and the action taken by the Holder Group pursuant to clause (A), (B) or (C) above (a “Cure Notice”), which Cure Notice shall also constitute the Founder’s written notice of such Sale for purposes of clause (2) above. No conversion of Class B Common Stock to Class A Common Stock pursuant to Section 2(c)(i) in respect of any Stapled Class C Shares Sold in violation of Section 2(a) shall be effective, and the Company shall not instruct the transfer agent to give effect to any such conversion, unless and until the applicable Cure Period has expired without such Sale having been cured in accordance with this Section 2(c)(iv), in which case such conversion shall be effective as of the close of regular trading on the last trading day of such Cure Period.
(d)Stapling Credits.
(i)The Holder Group shall accrue one (1) credit (each, a “Stapling Credit”) for (A) each share of Class A Common Stock or Class B Common Stock that is Sold by any member of the Holder Group and
(B) each share of Class B Common Stock that is converted by any member of the Holder Group into a share of Class A Common Stock, in each case at any time from and after the payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend, whether or not any Sale of Stapled Class C Shares is then contemplated; provided that no Stapling Credit shall accrue in respect of (1) any Sale or conversion made in satisfaction of Section 2(a)(i) or Section 2(c)(iv)(A) or (2) any conversion of shares of Class B Common Stock occurring pursuant to Section 2(c)(i). The automatic conversion of a share of Class B Common Stock into a share of Class A Common Stock upon its Sale pursuant to the Amended & Restated Charter shall give rise to one (1) Stapling Credit only.
(ii)Stapling Credits shall not expire and may be applied to any Sale of Stapled Class C Shares regardless of the period of time elapsed between the Sale or conversion giving rise to the Stapling Credit and such Sale of Stapled Class C Shares. Stapling Credits may be accrued, held and applied in fractional amounts.
(iii)Upon each Sale of Stapled Class C Shares by a member of the Holder Group that is not accompanied by a Sale or conversion pursuant to Section 2(a)(i) because the Founder has designated as such, Stapling Credits then accrued and not previously applied shall automatically be applied in accordance with Section 2(a)(ii) with respect to such Sale. A Sale of Stapled Class C Shares to which Stapling Credits are applied shall be deemed for all purposes of this Agreement to have been made in compliance with Section 2(a), and no conversion of shares of Class B Common Stock shall occur under Section 2(c) in respect of such Sale.
(iv)The number of unapplied Stapling Credits shall be equitably adjusted for any stock split, reverse stock split, combination, reclassification or similar event affecting the Class A Common Stock, Class B Common Stock or Class C Common Stock (other than any Additional Dividend), so that one (1) Stapling Credit continues to correspond to one (1) share of Class A Common Stock or Class B Common Stock.
3.Sales or Transfers of Class A Common Stock, Class B Common Stock and Class C Common Stock. Except as set forth in Section 4 and any applicable requirement set forth in the Amended & Restated Charter for a share of Class B Common Stock to convert to a share of Class A Common Stock (but subject to any exception to such conversion set forth in the Amended & Restated Charter), this Agreement shall not limit or restrict any member of the Holder Group’s ability to Sell or otherwise transact in any shares of Class A Common Stock, Class B Common Stock, or Class C Common Stock (other than Sales of Stapled Class C Shares).
4.Insider Trading Policy. The Founder shall cause each member of the Holder Group to comply with the Meta Platforms, Inc. Insider Trading Policy in effect from time to time (the “Insider Trading Policy”), with respect to the Class A Common Stock, the Class B Common Stock, the Class C Common Stock and all other Company securities.
5.Release from Stapling; Additional Dividends.
(a)Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, beginning on the last day of the first full calendar quarter that occurs following the payment of the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend (e.g., if the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend is paid on January 10, 2027, the first release of Stapled Class C Shares from the Sale restrictions as set forth in this Section 5(a) shall occur on June 30, 2027) and on the last day of each calendar quarter thereafter (each, a “Calendar Quarter”) in each case where such Calendar Quarter is a Qualifying Release Quarter (until there has occurred for thirty-two (32) Qualifying Release Quarters) a number of Stapled Class C Shares then held by the Holder Group equal to two and one-half percent (2.5%) of the number of shares of Class C Common Stock issued to the Holder Group in the Initial Class C Common Stock Dividend (as equitably adjusted for any stock split, reverse stock split, combination, reclassification or similar event affecting the Class A Common Stock, Class B Common Stock or Class C Common Stock (other than any Additional Dividend)) shall be released from the Sale restrictions set forth in Section 2 of this Agreement (allocated among the Holder Group as determined by the Founder from time to time) and, upon such release, shall be deemed to be Unstapled Class C Shares; provided that the failure of the Continued Leadership Condition shall not result in the forfeiture of, or otherwise affect the Holder Group’s ownership of, any share of Class C Common Stock, and that shares not released from the Sale restrictions set forth in Section 2 of this Agreement shall continue to be held by the applicable member(s) of the Holder Group and shall remain subject to Section 2. It is agreed that upon the thirty-second (32nd) Qualifying Release Quarter (which need not be over consecutive Calendar Quarters), all remaining Stapled Class C Shares then held by the Holder Group (equitably adjusted as noted above) shall be released from the Sale restrictions set forth in Section 2 of this
Agreement and shall be deemed to be Unstapled Class C Shares. In addition, notwithstanding Section 5(b) or anything else to the contrary in this Agreement, at the first time at which the Holder Group no longer holds any shares of Class B Common Stock (whether as a result of Sale, conversion or otherwise), (i) all Stapled Class C Shares then held by the Holder Group shall be released from the Sale restrictions set forth in Section 2 and shall be deemed to be Unstapled Class C Shares and (ii) from and after such time, no shares of Class C Common Stock held or thereafter acquired by the Holder Group shall be or be deemed to be Stapled Class C Shares for any purpose of this Agreement.
