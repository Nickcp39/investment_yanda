## Why the same company gets two different answers
The H float is 218M shares against 4,409M A shares; the H line carries a scarcity/flow premium that has run 30–40% since
listing. A buyer of H pays ~19x 2026E earnings for a claim that costs ~14x on the A line. The card therefore treats the
H/A premium as a **price condition in its own right** (≤ 15% before H becomes the preferred instrument) rather than
hoping the premium persists as a valuation support. For a holder who can only buy H, the honest statement is: the
business clears the hurdle at the A price and does not at the H price.

## What would move the band
- Up: Q4 gross margin ≥ 20% with NP ≥ 83B for 2026 (confirms Q3 was mix/tax, not price), an official export-rebate text that is milder than the reported 9%→6%, ESS share and price holding.
- Down: Q4 margin < 19%, 2027 guidance implying NP decline, net cash eroding below CNY 200B as inventory/receivables keep outrunning sales (9M OCF −24% while NI +32%).
