## runner_dissent

The bull case is not hard to write: the world's #1 battery maker, growing revenue 56%, net cash of CNY 260B, trading at 14x this year's earnings on the A line after a 45% drawdown, with the overseas and ESS legs both growing faster than the group. The Q3 "miss" was against a consensus that assumed margins would hold; profit still grew 14.5%. The honest counter is that this card is written for a holder who can only buy the H line, and the H line costs 35% more per unit of earnings than the A line for reasons (index flows, scarcity of the H float at 218M shares) that have nothing to do with the business. At 19x on a year whose Q4 will carry a new tax and a demand air-pocket, and with a 4% tax step and an unverified rebate cut in 2027, the hurdle is not met on H. On A it is roughly met today, and that is the line to underwrite properly next: operator underwriting (Zeng Yuqun), the value chain (lithium, LFP competition), and the moat map are not yet in this folder.
