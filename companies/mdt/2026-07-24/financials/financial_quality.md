## The Margin Bifurcation — Now PARTLY EXPLAINED (prior O4 largely resolved)

The prior dossier flagged the GAAP-up / non-GAAP-down divergence as its top unexplained financial-quality item. This run identifies concrete drivers of the non-GAAP pressure:
- **A $157M one-time charge** tied to future **Blackstone royalty/minimum payments** on the new insulin pump ("MiniMed Flex" financing) — an ~8c EPS hit, **explicitly NOT recurring in FY2027** [MEDTECHDIVE-MINIMED-DECON]
- **MiniMed IPO/separation dilution** (~4c IPO-timing + 1-2c/month) — transitional, tied to the ongoing deconsolidation
- **Tariffs** (~50bps FY / ~80bps Q4) — quantified at ~$185M, managed via 23 exclusions + manufacturing diversification

**Read**: the non-GAAP margin compression is largely **transaction/separation-related and tariff-driven, not a structural cost blow-out** — a materially more benign explanation than the "hidden cost problem" the divergence could have implied. The FY2026 non-GAAP EPS of **$5.53 landed inside the lowered $5.50-5.54 guide** MDT set on 2026-03-25 after the MiniMed IPO, confirming the reported result already absorbs these charges. This substantially de-risks the prior O4 flag (downgraded from "top open item" to "largely explained, monitor residual").

## New Structural Note — FY2027 Guide Includes Full-Year Diabetes; a Reset Is Coming

CFO Piéton (Q4 call): "Because we do not yet know the timing of the MiniMed separation, we're taking a conservative approach and **including the full year of the Diabetes business** in our estimates" (~25bps contribution); earlier separation = potential upside. So the FY2027 guide ($5.90-6.00 EPS, +6.75-7.25% organic) is a **conservative, Diabetes-inclusive** figure. When the split-off completes (~end CY2026), continuing-MDT revenue drops ~$3.1B and the EPS/dividend base recasts (shareholders receive MiniMed shares). This is a **known, non-adverse** structural reset — a modeling item, not a quality concern.
