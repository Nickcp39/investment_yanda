# MDT Business Model — REFRESH

Last updated: 2026-07-24 | as_of: 2026-07-24 | prior: `../2026-07-05/business_model.md` (full detail there; this notes the deltas)

---

## Model (unchanged in structure, sharpening in mix)

Medtronic is a diversified medical-device maker selling implantable and interventional hardware + consumables + (increasingly) software/AI into hospitals and physicians, on a razor/razor-blade dynamic (systems place, consumables/procedures recur), protected by physician training/preference switching costs and regulatory/reimbursement execution.

**Four reported segments (FY2026), transitioning to three:**
- **Cardiovascular** (~38% of revenue, +9.3% organic FY2026) — CRM, cardiac ablation (incl. PFA, +145% Q4), structural heart (incl. TAVR — under competitive pressure this period), coronary/peripheral, **renal denervation (Symplicity, now ~$100M annualizing)**
- **Neuroscience** (~28%, +3.1%) — spine, neuromodulation (**+ SPR/Sprint temporary PNS as of 2026-07-16**), specialty therapies
- **Medical Surgical** (~24%, +2.9%) — surgical innovations (**Hugo RAS + Touch Surgery Aide AI ecosystem**), general surgery, ENT, respiratory/GI/renal
- **Diabetes** (~9%, +7.9%) — MiniMed insulin pumps + CGM — **being deconsolidated via the MiniMed IPO (MMED, ~90% retained); full split-off ~end CY2026**

## What the Refresh Changes

1. **The B2B focusing is now real, not just planned**: MiniMed (the one B2C segment) is deconsolidating; post-split-off, continuing-MDT is a cleaner, more purely B2B device+consumable+software business.
2. **The mix is shifting toward higher-growth interventional/robotics/AI**: PFA, renal denervation, and the Hugo+AI ecosystem are the marginal growth drivers; the mature implant base (Neuroscience, most of MedSurg) grows 2-3%.
3. **A software/AI layer is emerging**: Touch Surgery Aide (NVIDIA-powered) + Instrument Exit Point (first FDA-cleared robotic-AI app) begin to add a data/AI/workflow layer on top of the hardware — a potential future recurring-revenue and lock-in vector, though not yet monetized separately.
4. **The recurring/consumable engine is compounding**: PFA catheters, RDN procedures, ablation consumables — installed-base expansion (per management) drives consumable pull-through. This is the razor-blade engine working.

## Unit Economics (unchanged read)

Gross margin ~65% GAAP; non-GAAP operating margin ~24-25% (under transitional MiniMed-separation + tariff pressure); FCF/NI 1.13x (strong cash conversion). Dividend King (49 yrs). The economics are those of a scaled, cash-generative, moderately-levered ('A' rated) device incumbent — durable, if not high-growth, with the growth-platform bets (PFA/RDN/Hugo) providing the upside optionality.
