## The Whole-Company Binding Constraint

**Unchanged: Hugo's commercial-scale bottleneck is the one that gates the CORE-vs-STARTER decision.** Every other platform's bottleneck either loosened (RDN reimbursement, MiniMed separation, neuromod funnel) or is a manageable competitive dynamic (PFA). The single constraint that has NOT loosened in a way that shows up in disclosed numbers is Hugo's US adoption speed / revenue — hospital capital budgets and physician retraining are slow, structural, and not something an AI platform or a 510(k) filing resolves in one quarter. Until that bottleneck visibly loosens (i.e., MDT discloses real Hugo revenue/placements), the thesis is capped at STARTER.

**Secondary emerging bottleneck**: TAVR competitive pressure on structural heart — a new constraint on the Cardiovascular segment that carries the growth story. Not yet binding (Cardiovascular +9.3%), but worth watching.
