## Kill Path 4: MiniMed Litigation Produces a Large Adverse Outcome (MOVED FAVORABLY — bounded + isolating)

**Substantially de-risked and reframed this period — the single biggest change in the inversion picture.**
- **Now quantified as bounded**: the FY2026 10-K discloses **fifteen (15) lawsuits on behalf of 55 individuals** (Series 600 retainer-ring), as of 2026-06-08. This is an *order of magnitude* smaller than the scary raw figures the prior dossier cited ("57,000 FDA complaints / 2,175 injuries / 1 death") — which are FDA MAUDE adverse-event *reports*, not lawsuits. The actual litigation surface is modest.
- **Now structurally isolating**: the MiniMed **Separation Agreement (per the S-1)** allocates the diabetes/insulin-pump product-liability matters to **MiniMed SpinCo**, with (uncapped) mutual indemnities. The prior dossier's specific worry — "unquantified litigation timed *awkwardly close* to the spinoff" — inverts: the spinoff is the **isolation mechanism**, not a complication.
- **Procedural posture is neutral-to-favorable**: no federal MDL formed; no global settlement; CA (LA Superior Court) bellwethers survived summary judgment + appeal and are moving toward trial; a separate MiniMed class action was decided in Medtronic's favor.

**Residual risk (why not fully closed)**: (a) MDT retains ~90% economic exposure until the full split-off completes (~end CY2026); (b) the indemnities are uncapped and only as good as MiniMed's (~$5B-cap) ability to pay; (c) deep-pocket-defendant risk — plaintiffs may still try to reach Medtronic directly for pre-separation conduct. Direction is clearly favorable; final isolation is not yet complete. LOWER risk than prior, on a path to resolution.
