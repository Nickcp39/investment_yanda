## Career-Relevant Note (Hugo vs ISRG vs China domestic robotics)

The most strategically interesting development for Nick's thread: Medtronic is trying to out-flank Intuitive not just on robot hardware (modular arms) but on an **open, NVIDIA-powered surgical-AI compute layer (Touch Surgery Aide)** + the first FDA-cleared robotic-AI app (IEP). If the competitive battle in surgical robotics moves from "whose robot is better" to "whose AI/data ecosystem is better," that is a layer where (a) Intuitive's hardware installed-base advantage matters less, and (b) Chinese domestic entrants (MicroPort MedBot/Toumai, Surgerii — **still not researched, still flagged as a gap**) would find it far harder to compete than on the robot alone. This is the mechanism that could convert Hugo from a me-too robot into a genuine second pillar — and it is precisely the thing that is NOT yet visible in revenue. Watch for the first Hugo/Touch-Surgery monetization disclosure.
