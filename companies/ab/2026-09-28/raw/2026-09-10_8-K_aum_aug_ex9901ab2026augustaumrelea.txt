 AB Announces August 31, 2026 Assets Under Management 
 Nashville, TN, September 10, 2026 - AllianceBernstein L.P. (“AB”) and AllianceBernstein Holding L.P. (“AB Holding”) (NYSE: AB) today announced that preliminary assets under management increased to $919 billion as of August 31, 2026, from $908 billion at the end of July. The 1.2% increase in AUM was driven by market appreciation, partially offset by net outflows. By channel, Institutional and Private Wealth generated net inflows, which were offset by net outflows in Retail.
