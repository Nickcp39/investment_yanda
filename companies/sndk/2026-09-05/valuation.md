## The question is not "is it cheap" — it is "what are normalized earnings"
Forward P/E of **8.13x** looks cheap. Trailing P/E is **23.59x**. Both are computed on the most extreme
earnings in the company's history, produced at an 84.6% gross margin. Twelve months ago the same company
ran a **30.08% gross margin and lost money**, and it lost money in **three of the last four fiscal years**.

At the 2026-08-13 Investor Day the company told investors it will hold **~80% non-GAAP gross margin and
~75% operating margin from FY2028 through FY2030**. That claim is the entire investment case.

**Price is 1.58x probability-weighted fair value.** The spread between bear and bull is roughly **10x** on
the same company at the same price. That is not a valuation disagreement — it is a binary bet on whether
the NAND cycle has been repealed.

## Buy-below: RAISED from $600 to ~$769
30% margin of safety against $1,098 probability-weighted fair = **$769**.

This is an **evidence-driven raise**, not a chase. FY26 actually delivered $73.76 of diluted EPS and
$11,494M of free cash flow; the June card could not model that because it had not happened. The entry line
moves up with the demonstrated earnings base. It is still **56% below the current price**.

## Why the entry stays conditional, not just numerical
The bull case cannot be confirmed in an up quarter. NBM contracts covering ~50% of FY2027 bits and ~2/3 of
FY2028 bits are a real structural change, but a contract raises the floor; nothing yet shows it holds the
80% ceiling once industry pricing turns. **STARTER requires price into ~$769–900 AND at least one quarter
of flat-to-down industry NAND pricing in which SanDisk's gross margin still holds well above its historical
mid-cycle range.** Price alone does not open it.

## The capital-cycle overlay (one-way, caps only)
SK Hynix approved **$38B** of new fabs on 2026-08-07 and fell 5% on its own announcement. Samsung and SK
Hynix are committing on the order of **$518B** to new Korean capacity. TrendForce has DRAM contract price
momentum decelerating **+90–95% → +58–63% → +13–18% → +3–8%** across 1Q26 to 4Q26F, NAND +10–15% in 3Q26.
Prices are still rising; the second derivative has turned while the capacity that answers these margins is
being financed. `caution = PEAK`. The 26.1% drawdown does not switch it off.

## One accounting flag carried into the model
FY26 capex was **$177M on $20,248M of revenue (0.9%)** because fab capacity sits in the Flash Ventures JV
with Kioxia. Reported FCF of $11,494M overstates the economic capital the business consumes. The bull case
above uses a 50% adjusted-FCF margin as the company frames it; if the JV mechanics load more capital onto
SanDisk than the capex line shows, the bull fair value is too high.
