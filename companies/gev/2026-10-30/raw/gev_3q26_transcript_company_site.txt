Acquiring Trench Group, a strategic addition 
The announcement today of our acquisition of the Trench Group is an important step forward 
in strengthening and scaling our Electrification business in the high and medium voltage 
markets globally. If I take a step back, the beginning of 2025, there were two assets of scale I 
felt we needed in Electrification. The first was Prolec to expand our North American 
manufacturing footprint and  to simplify the front -end commercial experience for our end 
customers, especially the hyperscalers. 
And the second is Trench for the technology differentiation, management team and the 
importance of the components Trench provides in solutions we deliver today. There are many 
factors driving our conviction in the high voltage market for the long term. Data centers require 
large electricity loads, driving an increasing need for more efficient, high voltage equipment. 
Trench, as an example, has secured multiple agreements with hyperscalers in the past two 
years. In addition, global utilities are also building out more high voltage infrastructure. Today's 
grid loses as much as 8% from power generation to end use, and the upgrade of the grid to 
higher voltages dramatically reduces energy losses, making the grid more economical. In 
addition, as the existing grid takes on more load with increased building electrification, 
manufacturing growth, heat pumps and EV's, grid reinforcements to higher voltages will be 
required. 
And, in parts of the world  building significant wind and solar, there is a need to transport 
electrons over long distances at higher voltages to eliminate waste. India is a great example of 
this today and is a market where we are demonstrating real commercial success, including our 
announced win on the Barmer HVDC project, that will be a $1.5 billion order in 4Q. Trench 
further positions us to serve this global high voltage market opportunity we project to be $130 
billion by the end of the decade.
