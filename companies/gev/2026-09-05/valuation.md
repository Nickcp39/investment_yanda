## The mechanical trigger fired. It is not being honoured. Here is exactly why.

941.95 is **0.85% below the prior card's $950 STARTER line** — the only entry trigger to fire anywhere in

### The error being corrected
The 2026-06-19 base case computed 2028 equity value as **2028 EV + ~$18B of projected "net cash"**, giving
~$974/share. That cash does not belong to shareholders. From the 2Q26 10-Q:

> H1-2026 cash from operating activities increased **$9.2B** YoY, driven primarily by an increase in
> **contract liabilities and current deferred income of $11.8B**, primarily due to higher **down payments
> on orders and slot reservation agreements** at Power, and higher down payments at Electrification.

It is customer float against **undelivered turbines**. Counting it as net cash while simultaneously
capitalising the future revenue it prepays double-counts the same economics.

### Independent arithmetic confirming it
FY26 revenue guidance $45.5–46.5B at the Q2 adjusted EBITDA margin of 11.3% implies roughly **$5.0–6.0B of
adjusted EBITDA**. The adjusted FCF guide is **$11.5–12.5B**. Free cash flow is running at about **2.2x
EBITDA** — arithmetically possible only through negative working capital. The headline **4.8% FCF yield is
a float yield**; on underlying earnings, EV of $241.87B against $5.0–6.0B of adjusted EBITDA is about
**44x**. The prior card's core objection at ~49x has barely improved despite the 15.1% price fall.
