## THE LOAD-BEARING FINDING — from the 2Q26 10-Q

> H1-2026 cash from operating activities increased **$9.2B** year over year, driven primarily by an
> increase in **contract liabilities and current deferred income of $11.8B**, primarily due to higher
> **down payments on orders and slot reservation agreements** at Power, and higher down payments at
> Electrification. The working-capital inflow was driven by contract liabilities and current deferred
> income of **$13.7B**.

**The raised FCF guidance is customer float, not earnings.** Independent arithmetic confirms it: FY26
revenue guidance of $45.5–46.5B at the Q2 adjusted EBITDA margin of 11.3% implies roughly **$5.0–6.0B of
adjusted EBITDA**, against an adjusted FCF guide of **$11.5–12.5B**. Free cash flow is running at about
**2.2x EBITDA**, which is only arithmetically possible through negative working capital.

## Consequence for the prior model
The 2026-06-19 valuation added **~$18B of projected 2028 "net cash"** to 2028 enterprise value in the
base case. That cash is a customer prepayment against undelivered turbines. Counting it as shareholder
net cash while also capitalising the future revenue it prepays **double-counts the same economics**.
Correction applied in `valuation.md`.

## INTERPRETATION
This is an accounting-quality problem, not a demand problem. Orders, backlog and the gas franchise are
the strongest in this batch. On the underlying earnings measure, EV of $241.87B against $5.0–6.0B of
FY26 adjusted EBITDA is roughly **44x** — the prior card's core objection at ~49x has barely improved
despite the 15.1% price fall.
