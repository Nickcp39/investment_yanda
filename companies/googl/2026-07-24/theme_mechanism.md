## The unchanged open question (why M2 strength ≠ buy)

M2 measures whether the *revenue mechanism* is durable and growing — it is, emphatically. It does NOT measure whether incremental revenue dollars survive AI-capex/D&A/SBC to reach owner earnings. That is M4's job, and M4 says the cash is being consumed by capex (FCF negative). So M2 stays at its +2 ceiling while M4 worsens — a coherent picture: **a demonstrably strengthening franchise that is, for now, plowing all of its (and more than its) cash back into the ground.**

Signal: **+2** (cannot go higher). The theme is the strongest part of the case; it is not the binding constraint.
