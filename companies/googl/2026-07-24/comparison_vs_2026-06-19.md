## Which PRIOR theses / tripwires MATERIALIZED in Q2

### MATERIALIZED (bear side)
- **F3 / K-B — the capex black hole (the headline).** June flagged K-B as 🟡 "on the line" (Q1'26 capex/OCF ~78% quarterly, 56% annual). Q2 delivered exactly what the tripwire was built to catch:
  - **Free cash flow NEGATIVE −$5.9B** (Q2), first negative quarter; **TTM FCF $53.3B, −20% YoY**.
  - **TTM capex/OCF 71.3%** — crossed the 70% line; **Q2 quarter 115%**.
  - **FCF/share $6.06 (FY25) → $4.36 (TTM), −28%**, while share count ROSE.
  - Buybacks still $0; **$49.6B equity + preferred issued; LT debt doubled to $98.2B**.
  - K-B is now **🔴 FIRING** (the strict "two consecutive fiscal years" clock formally closes at FY2026 year-end, but the mechanism is realizing now).
- **The owner-earnings-vs-reported gap widened, exactly as flagged.** June: net income inflated ~2–3x by a $36.9B equity gain. Q2: a **$99.0B** equity gain drove net income +298% ($6.26 of the $9.11 EPS) → reported now overstates owner earnings ~2.6–4.4x. The market saw through it (beat, but −7%) — validating the "use owner earnings, not net income" discipline.
- **Capex guide raised, no ROI framework (K-C).** June: "$180–190B, no visible return-discipline framework." Q2: **raised to $195–205B**, 2027 higher, and still only a qualitative "attractive return" from the CFO — no hurdle rate. K-C unresolved and worse.

### REFUTED / EASED (bull side — the honest other half)
- **F1 / K-A — search erosion: NOT happening.** June bear leg. Q2: **Search & other +17%**, management says AI features are *driving* query growth. K-A firmly 🟢 GREEN.
- **F2 / K-D — regulatory structural remedy: EASED.** June had it as a live tail. Since then the DOJ **search** case resolved **without a breakup** (behavioral, on appeal), and the DOJ **adtech** remedy judge is **skeptical of a structural divestiture**. K-D 🟢/🟡.
- **Incremental moat (the June "unproven" item): partly PROVEN.** Cloud held a 35.6% margin across a second quarter with a $513.9B backlog and tripling op income — the "can post-capex capital earn high returns?" test got its first real yes. This is the disconfirming evidence the June bear case explicitly said would break F3.

## Net: change or confirm?

**CONFIRM.** The two developments cut in opposite directions and roughly cancel for the *action*:
- Bear-confirming: FCF went negative (K-B fired), dilution/leverage rose, capex guide raised — the price is 14% lower *for a reason*.
- Bull-confirming: Cloud accelerated and proved margins, Search stayed strong, regulatory eased — the franchise is better, not worse.

The result is the same WATCH: at $317.69 the base-case 10y IRR is still negative (−1.0%) and only the bull case clears 8% (with no cushion), while the maintenance-vs-growth capex split — the one unobservable that decides everything — is now *more* binding because capex went up and free cash flow went negative. Buy-below nudges to ~$117. **Wait for price (~$99–117), not for a better story.**
