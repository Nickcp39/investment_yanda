## 0. One-line attack thesis (unchanged, now half-tested)

The cleanest permanent-impairment path was **F1 × F3 confluence**: AI answers compress search monetization (F1) *while* an ever-higher capex arms race (F3) means revenue grows but owner earnings are permanently diluted. **Q2 2026 split the two legs apart: F3 materialized hard (FCF negative), but F1 is being actively DISCONFIRMED (Search +17%, AI features additive).** A permanent impairment needs BOTH legs; one is failing. So the trap is now clearly a **capex/valuation trap, not a business-death spiral** — which is exactly why M5 holds at −1 (not −2).

## 2. What MATERIALIZED vs what was refuted (the honest scorecard)

- **MATERIALIZED — F3 (capex black hole):** the single most important development. Free cash flow went negative; the company is funding record capex by suspending buybacks, diluting (equity + preferred), and doubling debt. This is the realization of the June bear thesis and the K-B tripwire.
- **REFUTED / WEAKENED — F1 (search erosion) and F2 (structural regulatory remedy):** the two scariest *permanent* paths both got less likely. Search accelerated relative to fears; the DOJ search case ended without a breakup.
- **Net:** because the permanent-impairment combination (F1×F3) requires the search leg, and that leg is failing, the negative-FCF event reads as **an investment-phase cash drain with uncertain-but-improving return evidence (Cloud), not an irreversible franchise impairment.** Hence −1 (haircut/size driver), not −2 (veto).
