## Headline (release bullets, verbatim intent)
- Consolidated revenues increased 24% (23% cc) to $119.8B — 12th consecutive double-digit-growth quarter.
- Google Services revenues +15% to $94.5B (Search & other +17%, subs/platforms/devices +15%, YouTube ads +13%).
- Google Cloud +82% to $24.8B ("meaningful acceleration"), led by GCP enterprise AI Infrastructure + AI Solutions.
- Consolidated operating income +30%; operating margin +2pp to 34%.
- Other income net gain of $98.0B, "primarily the result of net unrealized gains on our equity securities."
- Net income available to common +298%; EPS +294% to $9.11.
- Pichai: AI features "driving Search query growth"; Gemini App 950M MAU; Gemini processes 22B API tokens/min;
  ~90% of Fortune 100 using Gemini Enterprise.
