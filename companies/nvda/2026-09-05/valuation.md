faster than the price. That is a genuine de-rating in the buyer's favour and is why M6 is scored -1, not -2.

## What has to happen before that band collapses to a point
The Q2 FY27 **10-Q**. If operating cash flow converges toward non-GAAP net income — i.e. the gap is a
working-capital inventory build ahead of the Q3 ramp rather than a structural change — the top of the band
is the right anchor and NVDA becomes the first credible STARTER candidate in this complex. If the gap is
structural, the anchor belongs near the bottom of the band and the stock is ~55% overvalued.

**Refusing to pick a point before that document is read is the whole discipline here.** Do not size on the
top of the band in advance.
