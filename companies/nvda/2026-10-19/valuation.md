## Why the band does not collapse to a point yet

The 09-05 rule: the top of the band is the right anchor **if** the OCF gap is a working-capital build that
reverses; the bottom is right **if** the gap is structural. The 10-Q says the gap is working capital — but of a
specific kind: **receivables from customers on 90-day-to-one-year terms, five of them ≥10% of AR**, in a
quarter where the market started to price customer credit. A working-capital build that reverses is a timing
question; a working-capital build that becomes an allowance is a cash-haircut question. One quarter cannot
tell them apart. The Q3 FY27 statement can: AR/quarterly-revenue was 0.66 at Q2 (0.50 at Q1); if it falls
back toward 0.45–0.50 with OCF/NI ≥ 70%, the top anchor holds.
