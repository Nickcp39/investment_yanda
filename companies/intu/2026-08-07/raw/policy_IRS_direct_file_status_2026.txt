1. IRS DIRECT FILE — CANCELLED FOR THE 2026 FILING SEASON
================================================================================
Source: Federal News Network, "IRS tells states Direct File 'will not be available' in 2026"
        https://federalnewsnetwork.com/it-modernization/2025/11/irs-direct-file-will-not-be-available-in-2026-agency-tells-states/
        Public date: 2025-11-05
Source: Tax Notes, "IRS Shutters Direct File, Citing Cost and Low Uptake"
        https://www.taxnotes.com/featured-news/irs-shutters-direct-file-citing-cost-and-low-uptake/2025/11/05/7t7q0
        Public date: 2025-11-05

QUOTED FROM THE REPORTING (the underlying document is an IRS email to state revenue departments,
dated 2025-11-03, from IRS product manager Cindy Noe):

    "IRS Direct File will not be available in Filing Season 2026. No launch date has been
     set for the future."

Treasury's stated rationale, per the same reporting: a combination of "low participation and
relatively high costs and burdens on the federal government." Treasury put the program's cost at
$41 million to operate for tax year 2024, i.e. at least $138 per return filed.

TIMELINE
  2024 filing season  — pilot, 12 states
  2025 filing season  — expanded to 25 states; 296,531 returns filed
                        (attributed to the Center for Taxpayer Rights)
  2025-11-03          — IRS notifies states: not available for Filing Season 2026
  2026 filing season  — Direct File not in market

SCALE CHECK (runner arithmetic)
  296,531 returns / ~160,000,000 US individual e-filed returns = 0.19%
  Even a tenfold survival would be ~2% of the market. Direct File was never large enough to
  explain a de-rating from 35x to 13.5x forward earnings.

CAPTURED LEAD — "FY2026 was Intuit's first tax season with no IRS Direct File"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: This is the RUNNER'S INFERENCE from the primary cancellation above, NOT a separately
sourced statement by Intuit or the IRS. The chain is: Direct File operated in the 2024 and 2025
filing seasons; it was cancelled for the 2026 filing season; Intuit's FY2026 (2025-08-01 to
2026-07-31) contains the 2026 filing season. Therefore FY2026 is the first Intuit fiscal year
since the program launched in which no IRS Direct File competed for returns.
It is stated as an inference wherever it appears in this dossier and is NOT entered in facts.md
as a company-disclosed fact.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE THESIS (the important part): the FY2026 tax season that management called
"not the overall tax season we expected" happened in a year when the government competitor had
been REMOVED. That makes the TurboTax unit and share loss HARDER to blame on policy, not easier.

2. LEGISLATIVE SUCCESSOR
================================================================================
Section 70607 of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act directs the Treasury Department to study
replacement models through public-private partnerships. Directionally this favours the
commercial preparers (Intuit's historical channel) over a government-built filer.
Runner note: the section number and its content are as reported by the B1 sources above; the
statutory text was NOT read this run.

3. INTUIT'S OWN RISK-FACTOR LANGUAGE (A1 — FY2025 10-K, filed 2025-09-03)
================================================================================
URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687825000035/intu-20250731.htm

VERBATIM:
    "Our consumer tax business also faces significant, increasing competition from the public
     sector, where we face the risk of federal and state taxing authorities implementing
     revenue-raising strategies that involve developing and providing government tax software or
     other government return preparation systems at public expense. These or similar programs
     have been and may continue to be introduced or expanded in the future, which may change the
     voluntary compliance tax system in ways that could cause us to lose customers and revenue.
     For example, the IRS made available a free direct filing system and has stated it will
     explore ways to expand eligibility for the program, including partnering with more states.
     Additionally, the legacy IRS Free File Program enables the IRS to offer free commercial tax
     software directly to qualifying taxpayers, and taxpayer adoption of this program could
     expand with increased awareness of and government support for the program."

NOTE ON DATING: this 10-K was filed 2025-09-03, i.e. two months BEFORE the 2025-11-03
cancellation. The risk factor is therefore STALE relative to what actually happened. The FY2026
10-K (expected ~early Sept 2026) should be read for revised language — a material softening
would be corroborating evidence for the "policy threat eased" reading.

Intuit's Q3 FY2026 press release still lists "potential governmental encroachment in our tax
business" among its forward-looking risk factors (A1, 2026-05-20), i.e. the company has not
declared the risk closed.
