2. **The leading indicator has already turned.** The industry wholesale price index is at record highs but just printed its **first month-over-month decline off the peak** [S003]. This is a funding-dependent business; if margins normalize while capital markets tighten, the outcome is forced dilution or worse — a large-magnitude downside, not a soft landing.

When primary supply heals, the demand pull-forward reverses at the same moment the pricing tailwind fades — a double whammy, not a durable acceleration.

The long-run secular story (low online penetration, share-taking DTC leader) may well be real — but that is a 5-10 year story.
